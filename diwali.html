Why do we celebrate Diwali?

Diwali is the festival of lights. Long ago, Lord Rama returned to Ayodhya after 14 years in exile. People lit diyas all over the city to welcome him home.

That is why we light diyas today: light wins over darkness, and good wins over evil.

We also worship Goddess Lakshmi, share sweets, and welcome the new year with family and friends.
